# McLaren F1 Team Overview\n\nMcLaren Racing Limited, trading as McLaren, is a British Formula One team based at the McLaren Technology Centre in Woking, England. Founded in 1963 by New Zealander Bruce McLaren, the team has won 9 World Constructors' Championships and 8 World Drivers' Championships.\n\n## Recent Successes\n\nIn 2024, McLaren has shown a strong resurgence with drivers Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri leading the charge. Norris has achieved multiple podium finishes and is in contention for the Drivers' Championship, while the team is battling for the Constructors' title against Red Bull and Ferrari.
